I am a fifth and final year PhD candidate in Finance at the London School of Economics, and until recently a PhD intern in the Financial Research Division of the Bank of England.

Before the PhD I worked at the European Central Bank, in the Risk Analysis division, monitoring the Public Sector Purchase Programme (PSPP) and the currency allocation of the ECB portfolio.

Research interests

Asset pricing, international finance and financial econometrics.
